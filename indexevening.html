Prices are climbing faster again

Two reports this week showed prices rising faster than experts expected. Wednesday's report covered what shoppers pay, and today's covered what businesses pay for goods, which was up 6.3% from a year ago. Some economists now expect the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates again. Stock markets were mixed today after a rough day yesterday.

What this means for you

You may keep noticing higher prices at the grocery store for a while. This is also the reason your Social Security raise is bigger this year, since it's tied to rising prices.

If interest rates do go up, savings accounts and CDs usually pay a little more, while loans and credit cards cost more. Nothing to do tonight, but this one is worth following.

Kentucky held its yearly earthquake drill today

Kentuckians were encouraged to take part in today's Great ShakeOut earthquake drill, part of Kentucky Earthquake Awareness Week. Western Kentucky sits near one of the most active earthquake zones in the central U.S., so the state encourages everyone to practice once a year.

What this means for you

There's no earthquake expected. This is just practice. If you missed it, the whole drill is three steps: drop to your hands and knees, cover your head under a sturdy table, and hold on until the shaking stops.

Texas Senate race breaks a fundraising record

Democrat James Talarico, running for U.S. Senate in Texas, announced today that his campaign raised $64 million over the summer, which his campaign says is a record for a Senate candidate at this point in an election year. Republican Ken Paxton is also getting big outside support, including $10 million from Elon Musk to a group backing his campaign.

What this means for you

Nothing changes for you in Kentucky. With this much money involved, though, expect to hear a lot about this race between now and November 3, and your family in Texas will see plenty of ads.
